# Reproducibility and editorial standards used to organize this archive

| Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [Scientific Data submission guidance](https://www.nature.com/sdata/submission-guidelines) | Distinguishes Data Descriptor structure from a research article; gives technical-validation requirements. |
| [PLOS Computational Biology submission guidance](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/s/submission-guidelines) | Data/code availability, validation and software paper expectations. |
| [Scientific Reports author instructions](https://www.nature.com/srep/author-instructions/submission-guidelines) | Article narrative, figures, supplementary information and declarations. |
| [FAIR principles](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618) | Rich provenance, discoverability, reuse and persistent metadata. |
| [National Academies reproducibility report](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/25303/chapter/7) | Complete input data, computational environments and executable methods. |
| [Nature Methods ML reproducibility](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-021-01256-7) | Source availability and third-party archiving. |
| [ICMJE authorship and AI disclosure](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/artificial-intelligence/ai-use-by-authors.html) | Human accountability and AI assistance reporting. |
| [RDKit force-field documentation](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/source/rdkit.Chem.rdForceFieldHelpers.html) | Force-field computational method context. |
| [ViennaRNA RNAfold reference](https://www.tbi.univie.ac.at/RNA/ViennaRNA/doc/html/man/RNAfold.html) | Example of a full thermodynamic RNA prediction implementation; no direct matched comparison asserted. |
| [RCSB PDB 1EHZ](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1EHZ) | Experimental reference source, not a validation benchmark the project has passed. |

The references are methodological/editorial context. No claim of affiliation, peer review, or endorsement is made.
