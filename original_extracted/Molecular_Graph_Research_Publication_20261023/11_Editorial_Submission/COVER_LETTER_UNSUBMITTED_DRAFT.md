# Cover letter for editorial consideration — draft, not submitted

**Manuscript:** Structural Sensitivity in a Native Spiking Neural Network Under Molecular Graph Interventions

Dear Editor,

We would like to present for editorial consideration a computational study examining whether selected molecular graph interventions produce reproducible differences in a native spiking neural network. The manuscript analyzes held-out graph-label classification, a cycle-preserving structural control, chemical-bond graph ablations, RNA-derived contact inputs, and force-field-derived contact representations. The original paired native recordings and source evidence are provided for independent audit.

The central reported finding concerns computational sensitivity to graph-connection changes. The manuscript explicitly distinguishes this outcome from experimentally validated RNA three-dimensional structure prediction, ligand affinity or medical outcomes. Non-significant and matched-control results are retained, and the accompanying archive documents the numerical reconstruction of the records.
