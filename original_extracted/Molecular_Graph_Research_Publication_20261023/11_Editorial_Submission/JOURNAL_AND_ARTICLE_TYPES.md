# What is submitted to a journal — and what is not

A **research article** reports a question, hypotheses, methods, results and scientific interpretation. The primary manuscript in this package is that article. A journal is a publication venue that evaluates a *submitted* manuscript through editorial and, if selected, peer review. Merely possessing a manuscript, figures or research archive does not mean the study is accepted or published.

A **Data Descriptor**, used for example by *Scientific Data*, serves a different purpose: the dataset's origin, format, acquisition methods, technical validation, and guidance for reuse. It is not appropriate to submit an interpretation-heavy second copy of this research article as a Data Descriptor. This package supplies a technical-validation and data-dictionary supplement instead.

A **methods/software paper** typically needs a precise innovation, tested algorithmic implementation, reproducibility documentation, accessible source, and an established target user community. The current evidence supports computational graph-intervention sensitivity, but does not include a task-matched experimental biological benchmark or independent review. Those remain separate scientific tasks.
