# Research submission checklist — not a submission receipt

This package contains research material ready for human author/editor review, not a peer-reviewed or accepted publication.

## Scientific completeness

- [x] Research question, computational hypotheses, controlled outcomes and interpretation in manuscript.
- [x] Raw computational evidence and source-derived figure provenance available in this archive.
- [x] Negative and non-significant controls are described.
- [x] Statistical accuracy is distinguished from response fractions and physical folding quality.
- [x] Original code archive, saved logs, and deterministic check tools retained.
- [ ] Independent statistical review signed off by a qualified researcher.
- [ ] Independent structural-biology review signed off.
- [ ] Task-matched external benchmark results, **if** biological prediction superiority is to be claimed.

## Editorial metadata (human author must verify; intentionally not fabricated)

- [ ] Confirmed author names, order, institutional affiliations and ORCIDs.
- [ ] Confirmed author contribution (CRediT roles) and final author approvals.
- [ ] Funding statement and funder influence, even if there was no funding.
- [ ] Competing-interests statement confirmed by every author.
- [ ] Confirm whether ethics approval is applicable to exact materials and procedures; no human or animal trial is asserted by this paper.
- [ ] Transparent acknowledgment and method description of any AI assistance used in writing, figure preparation, coding or analysis.
- [ ] Third-party code/data license check and source attribution.
- [ ] Permanent public data/code archive and DOI **only after actual deposit**.
- [ ] Select journal and adapt manuscript and figure limits to current journal requirements.
- [ ] Final cover letter signed by human corresponding author.

## Figure standards

The 14 delivered figures are already plotted from original numerical records. Before a particular journal submission, apply that journal's format and color/line-weight requirements without changing the plotted data. Figures must retain equations, denominators, sample units and distinctions between negative and positive controls.
