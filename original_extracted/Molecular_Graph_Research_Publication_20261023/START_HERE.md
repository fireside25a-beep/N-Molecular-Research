# Structural Sensitivity in a Native Spiking Neural Network Under Molecular Graph Interventions

**Research portfolio | Computational graph-intervention experiments | Evidence snapshot: 23 October 2026**

This package contains a computational research article, its numerical records and figures, a documented source-code archive, verification logs and submission-preparation materials. **It is not a peer-reviewed journal publication, and no journal submission or DOI is asserted.**

1. [Plain-language study explanation](03_Study_Guide/ABOUT_THE_STUDY.md) — what was asked, why, how, and what was established.
2. [Research article (PDF)](01_Research_Article/Research_Article.pdf) — formal introduction, methods, findings, controls, and limitations.

7. [Verification guide](07_Reproduce_And_Verify/README.md) — provenance verification and original-layout reconstruction.
8. [Journal-submission checklist](11_Editorial_Submission/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md) — documents and disclosures requiring human approval.

**Important:** "Accuracy" in the graph-label classifier is not RNA folding accuracy. Firing-mask disagreement is not molecular affinity, toxicity or a medical endpoint. Historic software tests and native runs are preserved evidence; they are not newly performed experiments in this edition.
