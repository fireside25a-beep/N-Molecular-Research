# Structural Sensitivity in a Native Spiking Neural Network Under Molecular Graph Interventions

This archive is a complete **computational research evidence portfolio**, not a claim of experimental folding accuracy or peer-reviewed acceptance.

**Read first:** `RESEARCH_MANUSCRIPT.pdf` (scientific paper), or `INDEX.html` (offline illustrated reader).

**Source and data:** `RESEARCH_MANUSCRIPT.tex`, `latex/portfolio_extended.tex`, `publication_methods/build_research_manuscript.py`, `figures_publication/`, `data/`, `COMPLETE_AUDIT_WORKBOOK.xlsx`, `evidence_and_logs/`, and `native_source_release/`.

**Reproducibility:** Run `python3 reproduce/VERIFY_PUBLICATION.py`. The core publication figure builder can regenerate the manuscript source and figures from the existing data; compiling TeX is a separate step (`pdflatex` twice in `latex/`). **Do not interpret these audit checks as a new native molecular simulation or biological validation.**

**Preservation:** The previous internally versioned research reports and original archives remain under `prior_portfolios/`, `supplement/`, and `native_source_release/`. Release IDs remain in raw evidence filenames solely to retain source paths. The public-facing manuscript is organized around experimental methods and findings.

**Primary audit totals:** 13,312 paired computational comparisons; 208 condition summaries; 100 archived graph-classification statistic rows; 81 historically recorded named tests. The initial graph study and later ablation studies have different endpoints; their scores are not pooled.

**Integrity:** `PACKAGE_SHA256_PUBLICATION.csv` gives a SHA-256 digest for each payload file and `RELEASE_INTEGRITY_REPORT.json` states exact completion checks. Older checksum reports are retained as history and should not be applied to altered publication files.
